# Spain compliance for pill imagery and copy

Sources: the Notion page "Spain Marketing Guidelines (WIP)" (Legal) and the Spain Launch Cheat Sheet, both as checked in October 2026. These rules change, so check the Notion page again before every build. This file is a summary, not legal advice. Legal signs off every page.

## First question: who sees the page?

The audience decides which rules apply, and the rules decide whether the object can look like medication at all.

| Audience | Rules |
|---|---|
| Public, paid ads, waitlist with no QC, QS with no QC, QC with no CP | **Marketing** rules (the same as ads) |
| CP with no OP, current and past patients, waitlist sign-ups who have done the QC | **Patient comms** rules |

A page showing a tablet, with words like "comprimido", "dosis" or "sin agujas", is patient communication. It can't go to public or paid traffic unless Legal approves it.

## Marketing rules: not allowed

- Drug or molecule names, and "GLP-1"
- Injection or pen words ("inyección", "pluma")
- "Receta", "prescribed", "prescription"
- "Weight loss pill" or "weight loss medication" ("píldora para adelgazar", "medicamento para adelgazar")
- Mechanism claims, such as controlling appetite or suppressing hunger
- Medication sale or delivery language, including "delivery to your door" ("a domicilio")
- Guaranteed results or any claim that hasn't been approved

Use instead: "doctor-led weight management programme", "targets your biology", "work with your body".

## Patient comms rules

Drug names, injection references and drug imagery are allowed. Keep the tone informative, not promotional.

## The tablet itself

- Generic and unbranded: no embossing, no maker's mark, no score line, no brand colours that match a real product
- Never pair the tablet with a drug name, a pack shot or a dose figure on a marketing page
- Dose and schedule copy (for example "uno al día, por la mañana") needs Medical to confirm it matches the Spain product information

## Language guide (Spain)

- "Doctor", not practitioner or clinician
- "Program" for the whole offering, "Treatment plan" for the clinical plan
- "Care Team" for patient support, "Health coaches" (not dietitian or nutritionist)
- "Medication partner" for IMA, never pharmacy or pharmacy partner

## Facts to check before quoting

- The money-back guarantee: the Spain site said 30 days and the cheat sheet said 25 days from first delivery. Leave it off until it's confirmed.

## Sign-off

Before anything goes live: Legal for the audience and the tablet visual, Medical for any dose or schedule wording. Run the restricted-term scan in `validation.md` and send the hits with their context.
